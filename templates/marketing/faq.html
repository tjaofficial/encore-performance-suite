Getting Started

Starting with Encore

What is Encore Performance Suite?

Encore is studio-management software built for dance and performance studios. It connects students, families, instructors, classes, attendance, registration, communication, tasks, reporting, choreography, costumes, and other studio workflows.

Who is Encore designed for?

Encore is designed for dance and performance studios that need a modern system for both day-to-day administration and the creative work surrounding classes and performances.

Do I have to book a demo before signing up?

No. You can create your studio account and subscribe online whenever you are ready. Demos are available for studios that want a guided walkthrough or help deciding what fits.

Is there a free trial?

No. Encore begins billing when you complete registration and secure checkout. Your studio account activates after payment.

When does billing begin?

Billing begins when your studio completes signup and checkout. Encore subscriptions are billed monthly.

Can Encore help me switch from another studio platform?

Yes. Encore can help plan onboarding and migration based on your current system, your studio size, and the data you need to bring over.

Can I add staff after I create my studio?

Yes. Administrators can add staff access as the studio is configured and ready to invite instructors or other administrators.

Pricing & Subscription

How billing works

How is Encore priced?

Encore uses student-record pricing bands combined with two software tiers: Essentials and Pro. Each tier also has a lower Software + Payments price when Encore Payments is enabled.

What counts as a student record?

Every non-deleted student record associated with the studio counts, including inactive and historical student records.

What happens when my studio moves into another pricing band?

Encore automatically schedules the correct student-record band for your next billing cycle. The change does not unexpectedly reprice the middle of your current cycle.

Will Encore tell me before my subscription price changes?

Yes. Encore creates an in-app subscription notice and sends an email when a student-record band change is scheduled.

Can I upgrade from Essentials to Pro?

Yes. Upgrades take effect immediately and the current billing period is prorated.

Can I downgrade from Pro to Essentials?

Yes. Downgrades are scheduled for the next billing cycle so you keep your current Pro access through the period you already paid for.

Can I cancel Encore myself?

Yes. Studio administrators can schedule cancellation from Subscription & Billing without contacting sales.

What happens after I cancel?

Your studio continues to have access through the end of the current billing period. The subscription then stops renewing.

Can I change my mind after scheduling cancellation?

Yes. Before the subscription ends, you can choose “Keep My Subscription” and remove the scheduled cancellation.

What if my subscription payment fails?

Encore does not immediately shut the studio down. The system supports payment retries, warnings, email and in-app notices, and a grace period before access is restricted.

Is annual billing available?

Not at launch. Encore is billed monthly.

What is Software + Payments pricing?

Studios using Encore Payments receive a lower monthly software price. That discount applies while Encore Payments is enabled and connected.

Features

What Encore can do

What is included in Essentials?

Essentials includes the complete core studio-management foundation: students and families, instructors, classes and seasons, enrollment and waitlists, scheduling, attendance, basic calendar, tasks and reminders, announcements and notifications, family, teacher, and student portals, basic email sending, registration, basic reports, and studio branding.

What does Pro add?

Pro adds advanced and differentiating operations including evaluations, task templates and automation, advanced calendar, teacher time-off requests, substitute management, email templates, signatures, campaigns, scheduling, analytics and automation, birthday email, costumes, measurements, music, dance and choreography management, dashboard customization, advanced reporting, and multi-location management.

Does Encore support multiple locations?

Yes. Multi-location management is part of Encore Pro.

Can teachers take attendance?

Yes. Teachers can access class rosters and record attendance from their workspace.

Do families have their own portal?

Yes. Encore includes role-aware family, teacher, and student portal experiences.

Can students have their own accounts?

Yes. Encore supports a student workspace in addition to family and teacher workspaces.

Can Encore manage costumes and measurements?

Yes. Costumes and student measurements are part of Encore Pro and are designed to stay connected to performance workflows.

Can teachers request time off?

Yes. In Pro, teachers can request one or more dates off and administrators can review the classes affected by each date.

Can administrators assign substitute instructors?

Yes. Pro supports substitute assignment for individual class occurrences with schedule and time-off conflict checks.

Does Encore include email campaigns?

Pro includes advanced email functionality such as templates, signatures, campaigns, scheduling, analytics, and automation.

Can Encore send birthday emails?

Birthday email is part of Encore Pro's automated email tools.

Can users customize their dashboards?

Yes. Dashboard customization is a Pro feature.

Drawing Board

Visual choreography planning

What is Drawing Board?

Drawing Board is Encore's premium visual choreography and formation-planning workspace. It is designed to help studios plan dancers, formations, stage pictures, and performance movement inside the same system used to manage the studio.

Is Drawing Board included in Pro?

No. Drawing Board is a separate premium add-on so studios can choose it independently of their main Encore tier.

How much does Drawing Board cost?

Drawing Board is $39 per month as a flat studio add-on.

Can Essentials studios use Drawing Board?

Yes. Drawing Board can be added to either Essentials or Pro.

Is Drawing Board priced per teacher or per dance?

No. The current pricing is one flat $39 monthly add-on for the studio rather than a per-teacher or per-dance fee.

Can I remove Drawing Board later?

Yes. Removal takes effect at the next billing cycle, so you retain access through the current paid period.

Payments

Encore Payments

Can families pay through Encore?

Encore Payments is being built to support family payments directly through the platform. Final payment-processing rollout and rates will be published separately.

Will Encore support cards and ACH?

Card and ACH support are part of the planned Encore Payments architecture. Final processing pricing is still being modeled.

Can I use Encore without Encore Payments?

Yes. Every pricing band has a Software Only option.

Does Encore support recurring tuition?

Encore has a recurring tuition billing foundation tied to class enrollment and studio billing policies. Payment processing through Encore Payments is a separate rollout.

Can I record cash, check, credits, and other payments?

Encore's billing foundation supports recording family-account payments and credits in addition to future online processing.
