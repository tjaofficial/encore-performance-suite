Calculation Foundation

Payroll Calculation Rules

Scheduled Teaching Class meeting duration + minimum paid session.
Approved Time Off Will remove payable occurrences when applicable.
Substitute Coverage Will move payable time to the instructor who covered.
Extra Work Will add logged work using the applicable rate.

These rules now feed Encore's live payroll calculation engine. Rate history keeps past seasons tied to the compensation that was actually effective on each work date.
