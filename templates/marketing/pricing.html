What counts as a student record?

Every non-deleted student record associated with your studio counts, including inactive and historical student records.

↗

What if my studio grows?

Encore automatically moves you to the correct student-record band at the next billing cycle and sends an in-app notice and email about the upcoming price change.

↔

Can I change plans?

Yes. Upgrades take effect immediately with proration. Downgrades take effect at the next billing cycle.
